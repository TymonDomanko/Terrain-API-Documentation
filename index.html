1. The levels

Terrain organises everything as a tree. Pick a level to filter the call list below. Most reporting data lives at unit level, because one call returns every member of a unit.

5. Rules and gotchas
Which award date to use: Terrain's own award date, the top-level date_awarded, wherever a record has one. Fallbacks, in order: imported.date_awarded, then status_updated. Anything that reads only imported.date_awarded and status_updated will show different dates for records recorded in Terrain, so check which field a report uses.
